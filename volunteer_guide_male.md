# SheAlert Volunteer Recording Guide — For Male Volunteers

Hi, and thank you for even considering this. We're a student team from Air University,
Islamabad, building SheAlert — an app that quietly listens in the background and
recognises when someone might be in danger, so it can call for help automatically,
even if she can't reach her phone.

For the app to recognise real distress, it first has to learn what genuine urgency
sounds like in Urdu — and that's where you come in. This page tells you exactly what
your recording session involves, so you know precisely what to expect before you decide.

## Your session at a glance

- You'll be given an anonymous ID such as M01 — one of 20 male volunteers taking part.
- You'll record 30 short clips in total, across the three parts below.
- The whole session takes about 15 minutes, done in one sitting, in a quiet space on
  campus.

## What you'll record

Your session has three short parts, and you'll be guided through each one, line by
line — nothing to prepare or memorise in advance.

### Part 1 — Words people say when asking for help (12 clips)

You'll say each of these 4 words three times — getting a little more urgent each time.
That's 4 words × 3 takes = 12 clips. It's the same idea as reading a line for a
scene — you're acting out the urgency, not feeling it.

| Word | Meaning | Take 1 (Mild) | Take 2 (Urgent) | Take 3 (High) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bachao | Save me / Help | Calm, slightly worried | Louder, clearly urgent | Loud, fast, strong urgency |
| Madad karo | Help me | Calm, slightly worried | Louder, clearly urgent | Loud, fast, strong urgency |
| Choro mujhe | Let go of me | Calm, slightly worried | Louder, clearly urgent | Loud, fast, strong urgency |
| Help | Help (English word) | Calm, slightly worried | Louder, clearly urgent | Loud, fast, strong urgency |

### Part 2 — A normal conversation (9 clips)

We'll ask 9 easy, everyday questions and you just answer naturally, in your own words,
for about 5 to 7 seconds each — like you would with a friend. Nothing to script or
rehearse.

| Topic | Question |
|---|---|
| Daily routine | How was your day today? |
| University | What time is your class? |
| Food | What's your favourite food? |
| Weather | How's the weather today? |
| Shopping | What did you buy last week? |
| Travel | How do you usually get to university? |
| Friends | What do you do with your friends? |
| Studies | What's your favourite subject? |
| Hobbies | What do you like to do in your free time? |

### Part 3 — A few firm, stern-sounding lines (9 clips)

You'll read each of these 9 lines once, in a cold, commanding tone — again, just
acting, the way a character in a drama would deliver them. Lines marked High are said a
little louder and faster.

| Line | Meaning | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Chup ho jao, warna theek nahi hoga | Be quiet, or it will not go well | Medium |
| Zyada mat bolo, chalo mere sath | Do not talk back, come with me | Medium |
| Tumhe pata nahi main kya kar sakta hoon | You do not know what I can do | High |
| Shor mat karo | Do not make noise | Medium |
| Ab bhaagne ki koshish mat karna | Do not try to run now | High |
| Khabardar, ek qadam bhi mat uthana | Careful, do not take a single step | High |
| Jo kaha hai wohi karo | Just do what I said | Medium |
| Tumhe samajh nahi aa rahi? | Do you not understand? | Medium |
| Yeh aakhri baar keh raha hoon | I am saying this for the last time | High |

## It's all acting

Every part of this is performance, not a real scenario. Nothing dangerous happens
before, during, or after your session. Think of it the same way an actor reads a line
for a film — you're simply giving your voice, not living out an event.

## Your privacy and your choice

- Your name is never attached to any recording — only your anonymous ID.
- Your voice clips are used only to help train the app's safety model — nothing else.
- You can stop at any point, skip anything you're not comfortable with, and you don't
  need to give a reason.
- Even after your session, you can ask us to delete all of your recordings at any
  time — just tell us your ID.

## What happens when you show up

1. You'll meet one of us, get a quick, friendly explanation of what to expect, and sign
   a short consent form.
2. We'll record a 5-second test clip together, just to check the phone's audio is clear.
3. We'll go through the three parts above at your own pace.
4. You're free to go — and you've directly helped make a safety tool for women.

We'd genuinely appreciate your help. If you're interested or have any questions, just
reach out to a SheAlert team member — we're happy to explain more before you decide
anything.
